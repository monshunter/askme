# Lighthouse Reliability Console

Taylor Chen led the design and delivery of Lighthouse Reliability Console, an internal incident-response product for a distributed platform team.

The product was implemented with TypeScript, Next.js, PostgreSQL, and OpenTelemetry. Taylor designed the incident timeline model, built evidence-linked service views, and introduced idempotent ingestion for alert events.

In a six-week measured pilot, Lighthouse reduced median incident triage time by 31 percent and cut duplicate alert investigations by 24 percent. The platform team reviewed the measurements against incident records before adopting the workflow.

Taylor coordinated product, SRE, and security reviewers, documented recovery procedures, and added failure-state dashboards so operators could distinguish delayed ingestion from missing evidence.
